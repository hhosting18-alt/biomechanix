Safety, built in

It knows when it shouldn't give a workout.

Type “my knee hurts when I climb stairs” into MoveMentor Fitness. Instead of a squat routine, it spots the pain and offers a physio or a guided movement check.

A rep counter would have counted the squats.

  • Adaptive coaching eases the target when a rep is out of reach.
  • Stress view shows the load on working joints as you move.
The part most movement apps don't explain

Your camera video stays on your phone.

The camera is read live on your phone and nothing it sees is stored — no images or video leave your device. Sign in, and only results sync: reps, scores and answers, shared only with people you choose.
